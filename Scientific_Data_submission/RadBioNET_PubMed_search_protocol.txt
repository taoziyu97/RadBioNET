RadBioNET archived PubMed search specifications

Source material
Project workflow presentation 数据库开发流程.pptx, slides 4, 5 and 7.
These blocks transcribe the archived search specification. Historical PubMed
Search Details and exact run timestamps were not recovered. The preserved
NBIB exports are the reference inputs for the manuscript's archive counts.

Transcription
Smart quotation marks were converted to straight quotation marks. Spaces were
restored around the concatenated OR operators in the focused proton block.
The four general date clauses shown on one slide are presented as separate
alternatives, matching the four retained general export filenames. The broad
proton slide states a 2000–2026 publication-year range in prose. The focused
proton slide does not display a separate publication-date clause.
